## Full Description

Lemuel brings you a daily proverb from the book of Proverbs — one chapter at a time. No cherry-picked verses. Just the full wisdom of each passage, the way it was written.

It's not just reading. It's reflecting.

1. Read — Each day brings a new proverb. Choose from multiple Bible versions.
2. Meditate — Stop and sit with the verse. A full-screen animated meditation timer helps you focus without distractions.
3. Journal — Capture your thoughts in a rich-text editor. Your notes are saved per proverb and travel with your account.
4. Share & see — Optionally read what others are reflecting on. Community notes let you see a proverb through different eyes.

More features:

- Multiple Bible versions supported — including the NIV and KJV.
- Daily reminders — Choose a fixed time or a random window.
- Home screen widget — The day's proverb is always visible, updating automatically.
- Your stats — Track your meditations completed and notes written over time.
- Free. No ads. No noise.

Lemuel is designed for one thing: helping you slow down, chew on a piece of wisdom, and let it settle. Not another scroll — a pause.
